CapyCake

Bake cakes, biscuits and cupcakes with a little capybara chef.
Sit or stand about 1 metre from the webcam so it sees both hands.

  • Fist on an ingredient = grab it · open your hand = drop it
  • Clap = crack an egg · shake = whisk · circles = stir
  • No webcam? Play with the mouse (click and hold = grab, Space = clap).

The browser will ask to use your camera. Video never leaves this computer.
